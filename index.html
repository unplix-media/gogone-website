Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers about Gogone and our apps.

What is Gogone?

Gogone is an independent Android developer that publishes simple, useful utility and personalization apps on Google Play.

Where can I download the apps?

All Gogone apps are available on Google Play. Tap any app card above, or visit our developer page.

Are the apps free to use?

Please check each app's Google Play page for current pricing, ads and in-app purchase details.

Why do some apps ask for permissions?

Apps only request permissions needed for their main feature, for example the microphone for Voice Lock. You can read more in our Privacy Policy.

How do I report a bug or suggest a feature?

Email us using the contact section below, or leave a review on the app's Google Play page. We read every message.

Which Android versions are supported?

Supported Android versions differ for each app. The requirements are listed on every app's Google Play page.
