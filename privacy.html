Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Gogone ("we", "us") handles information in the Android apps we publish on Google Play (the "Apps").

Information we collect

Our Apps are designed to work on your device. We do not ask you to create an account and we do not sell your personal information. Some Apps may request device permissions (such as microphone, camera, storage or overlay) only to provide their core feature — for example, Voice Lock uses the microphone to recognise your voice unlock phrase, and Transparent Live Wallpaper uses the camera to show the see-through effect. This data is processed on your device.

Third-party services

Some Apps may use third-party services, such as Google Play Services, Google AdMob and Firebase Analytics, which may collect information like device identifiers, ad identifiers and usage data according to their own privacy policies:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • AdMob
  • Firebase

Children's privacy

Our Apps are not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information, but no method of electronic storage is 100% secure.

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page.
